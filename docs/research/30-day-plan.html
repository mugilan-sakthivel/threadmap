30-day build-in-public plan → launch with payments

Live conversation map for podcast hosts · 25 Oct 2026

1. The core idea: the product is the content

Most SaaS demos are boring screen recordings. Yours isn't: a diagram drawing itself while people talk is naturally watchable. So the growth engine is simple: run the product on famous podcast conversations and post the split-screen (podcast on the left, map building itself on the right). Every video is a demo, every video borrows the podcast's attention, and you can tag the host. That's the loop.
  • The hook format: “I made AI map [famous episode] live while they talked.”
  • The outreach loop: send the host their episode's map for free → they repost it → their audience includes other podcasters → signups.
  • Your unfair angle: Tamil/Hindi + English mixed. “Every AI notes tool breaks on Tanglish. Mine doesn't.” That's a story Western founders can't tell.

4. Videos that can go viral

Honest note: nobody can guarantee viral. What you can control is posting a clearly visual, hook-first video 3× a week for 4 weeks. One of ~12 videos usually breaks out. Optimize for consistency, then double down on whatever format hits.

Production kit (simple)

  • Record: Screen Studio or OBS (screen) + phone for face-cam.
  • Edit: CapCut: auto-captions (always on: most people watch muted), 9:16 vertical for Reels/Shorts, 16:9 for X/YouTube.
  • Rules: hook in the first 2s · map must be moving within 3s · under 45s · end with “link in bio / comment MAP for access”.
  • Batch: record 3 videos every Sunday, edit Monday, schedule for the week. Daily text posts are quick.

Using famous podcast clips safely

  • Use short clips (under ~30–45s), always credit the show, add your own commentary/visual (transformative), and take down on request. It's a gray area: best is to ask the host first, which doubles as outreach.
  • Safest source: record your own conversations with friends/founders. Those are 100% yours.

5. Launch day (Day 28)

  1. Morning: email the waitlist (“we're live, founding price ends in 72 hours”).
  2. Product Hunt launch (prepared on Day 26–27), plus X thread with launch video pinned.
  3. LinkedIn post (strong for Indian founders and podcasters), Peerlist launch, Indie Hackers post.
  4. Reddit: r/podcasting, r/SideProject. Share as a story/feedback request, not an ad.
  5. DM every beta host and every host you mapped: “we're live, would mean a lot if you shared.”
  6. Stay online all day: reply to every comment within minutes. That drives the algorithm and conversions.

Launch offer

  • “Founding 100”: $9/mo or ₹399/mo locked forever (normal price $15 / ₹599). Creates urgency and rewards early fans.
  • No lifetime deals. Every recorded hour costs you real money (STT + LLM), so lifetime plans lose money.
  • Free tier: 2 hours/month so people can try it on one episode.
  • Start onboarding with the payment provider on Day 1. KYC/approval can take days to weeks.

8. What can go wrong

  • Scope creep kills 30-day plans. Overlay, teams, and events mode are not this month.
  • Map quality is the product. If the map looks messy, the videos won't land. Spend Week 1 getting the layout calm and readable.
  • Payments approval delay: apply on Day 1 and have Razorpay as backup.
  • Burnout: one rest/buffer day per week is built in. Keep it.
  • Cost spikes from a viral moment: hard per-user hour caps and silence auto-pause before launch.
