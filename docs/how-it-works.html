What happens in the ~2–4 seconds after someone speaks

0 – 0.3sWords appear

Soniox streams text while they talk, shown grey in the transcript.

+0.5 – 1sSentence final

A pause or speaker change locks the line (solid text).

+1.5sBatch

Waits for a short pause so the AI sees whole thoughts (max 6s in non-stop speech).

+0.5 – 2sAI decides

Returns notes + small drawing steps like “fill slot 2 = Honest”.

<0.05sDrawn

Code places it with a fixed template. The camera follows.

The AI never draws or positions anything. It only says what (“this is a pyramid”, “add Honest”). Code decides where, so the board stays tidy and never jumps around.

Frame by frame (real screenshots from the running app)

1A question opens a section
Host: “So first, how did you actually start Kanavu Naturals?”

AI → start_section "How did you start?". A numbered question pill appears at the top of column 1.

2A spoken shape becomes that shape
Guest: “Think of it like a pyramid. Foundation is a product people actually love…”

AI → create_visual pyramid + fill slot 0 (base). The base is drawn and the upper levels wait dashed. Pink means “being drawn right now”.

3It fills as they keep talking
“Then on top of that, repeat buyers… and only at the top, brand.”

Each sentence fills the next level. Meanwhile the notes panel pinned a follow-up: she mentioned a “₹40L mistake” but didn't explain it.

4“There are four methods” → 4 empty boxes instantly
“There are four methods we used. First, Instagram reels…”

AI → create_visual grid, capacity 4. All 4 slots show at once (dashed) and turn solid one by one as each method is explained.

5Next question → next section, below
Host: “And what does a good product mean for you?”

Section ③ starts under section ②, separated by a dashed line. Sections stack top → bottom.

6No count announced: “effective, efficient, honest…”
“It has to be effective… And efficient, one product instead of five… And honest, full ingredient list.”

Nobody said “three things”. The AI sees parallel qualities and draws a grid without a fixed size. A single word (“effective”) was only a note. As soon as a second and third arrived, it became a diagram. Section ③ no longer fit column 1, so it moved to a new column on the right.

7They add one more later → it grows
“Oh, and affordable also, under five hundred rupees.”

AI → fill_slot into the same grid (no new diagram). It grows to 4.

The finished board: two columns, five questions

Column 1: pyramid + 4 methods. Column 2: the unannounced “good product” grid, the mistake as steps, and Bootstrap vs VC as a comparison. After “End session” the canvas becomes editable and you can download it as a PNG.
